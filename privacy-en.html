Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026

bocch (the “Service”) is a board game you can play in your browser. The operator of the Service (the “Operator”) handles users' information as follows.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

  • Account information: if you create an account, we keep your email address and your name, rating and game record. We use them only for signing in and for carrying your records across devices.
  • Game records: in online games and when watching, the moves, names and ratings in a room are sent to a relay server. This is needed for the game to work, and the records are deleted automatically after at most 12 hours.
  • Information stored on your device: your game history, review analysis results, name, settings and similar data are stored on your device (in your browser). They are not sent to the Operator.

We do not give the information we collect to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

2. Advertising

The Service uses (or plans to use) the third-party advertising service “Google AdSense.” Google and other ad vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to the Service and other websites, in order to show ads that match your interests.

  • For how Google uses cookies in advertising, see Google's Policies and Terms.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings.

3. External services we use

  • Google Firebase (account management and record storage)
  • Cloudflare (relay for online games)
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of the Service)
  • Google AdSense (ad delivery)

How each service handles information is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.
